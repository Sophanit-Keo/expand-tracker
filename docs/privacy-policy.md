# Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Expense Tracker ("the app") is a personal budgeting app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

**Your data stays on your phone.** The app has no account, no servers, no ads and no analytics. It doesn't have permission to use the internet, so it can't send your information anywhere.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter or import is saved only in the app's private storage on your device:

- expenses and income: amount, category, date and note
- bank statements you import: the transactions you choose to keep, plus each statement's dates, totals and opening/closing balances
- your settings: currency, monthly budget, theme, and the categories you picked for merchants

## Bank statement files

When you import a statement (CSV, Excel or PDF), the file is read on your device. The file itself isn't copied or kept. If the PDF is password-protected, the password is used only to open it and is never saved.

## Sharing

The app doesn't sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Data only leaves the app when **you** choose to export it ("Export as CSV"), and it goes to the app you pick in Android's share menu.

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your personal backup to your Google account. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Delete all expenses** removes everything you've entered or imported.
- **Settings → Remove imported transactions** removes only data imported from bank statements.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.

## Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13.
